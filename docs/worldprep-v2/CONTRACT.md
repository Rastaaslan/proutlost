# WorldPrep V2 production contract

WorldPrep is a **zero-touch environmental compiler**. Its production flow is an
immutable source, a verified disposable build, a sealed deterministic plan,
transactional application, validation, and a prepared candidate. Preview and
planning are read-only and never generate chunks. Apply and rollback use only
existing, accepted-status chunks and an exact sealed pipeline identity.

River owns handmade topology before compilation and artistic or narrative builds
after compilation. WorldPrep owns terrain analysis, biome distribution, geology,
ores, soils, aquatic preparation, eligible environmental structures, flora,
trees, habitat classification, and validation. Narrative cities, story locations,
World Director, seasons, and story runtime are excluded.

Uncertainty fails closed as `CLEAN_REFUSAL` or `RECOVERY_REQUIRED`; it never
becomes best-effort mutation. The only successful uncertain no-change result is
`SAFE_NOOP`. No public destructive path may bypass the enabled gate, immutable
workspace, sealed-plan validation, chunk preflight, durable journal, ownership,
or three-state mutation semantics.
